# Fueling and hydration

- Sessions over 90 minutes: practise 30-60 g of carbohydrate per hour; ultra preparation trains the gut towards 60-90 g per hour.
- Rehearse the race-day fueling and drinking plan on the last two or three long runs.
- Drink to thirst, about 400-800 ml per hour depending on heat; add sodium on long or hot sessions.
- Never prescribe a race-weight or weight-loss target; low energy availability harms health and performance (RED-S).
- Plan race-week carbohydrate loading only for races over 90 minutes.

## Race nutrition plan

The race's Nutrition tab shows the plan Stride allocates from the race targets and the athlete's products; `read_race_nutrition` returns it.

- Targets: 60-90 g of carbohydrate per hour for ultras, 40-60 g for shorter races, only above what the athlete has tolerated in training when it was rehearsed; water 400-800 ml per hour by heat; sodium 300-600 mg per hour, more in the heat.
- Allocation: Stride counts whole servings per segment from the moving time, fills the flasks at each station that has water (drink flask first, then water), keeps caffeinated products for night segments within the caffeine maximum, and packs everything after the first base into the drop bag.
- Change a segment only when the allocation misses something the athlete needs: a product they tolerate better, solid food on a long climb, a compote before a hard section. Give exact counts by product id.
- Race day uses products tested in training. Name the untested ones and the long session where to try them.
- The drop bag extras are things to leave at the base besides food, such as a warm jacket, dry socks or a spare headlamp.
