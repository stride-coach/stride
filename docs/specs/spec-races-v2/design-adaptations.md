# Races design adaptations

The `V2 · Courses` artboards of the [design board](../../design/design-board.md) remain the visual source of truth. These adaptations apply only where an accepted product decision or missing data cannot support the mockup. Everything else the board draws and the app lacks is tracked under epic #513.

| Screen                       | Mockup element                                                                                   | Implementation                                                                                                                                                                                | Reason                                                                                                             |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Ajouter une course · 3       | Before → after days, impact tiles and « Accepter et ajouter la course » before anything is saved | The race is saved at step 2. Step 3 asks the coach for the plan change and follows it; the before/after weeks, validator results and accept or refuse live in the plan proposal review (#534) | Only the coach writes plan revisions; Stride has no deterministic generator to preview a change synchronously      |
| Plan de course and its views | Effort lane, « FC à ne pas dépasser », heart-rate setting, FC max in the roadbook and the watch  | No heart-rate lane, ceiling or alert                                                                                                                                                          | No heart-rate source for a race ceiling yet ([information architecture](../../design/information-architecture.md)) |
| Plan de course               | Individual intakes, caffeine and missed intakes on the Ravitaillement lane                       | Carbs per section on the lane; intakes stay on the Ravitaillement tab                                                                                                                         | Accepted in the [information architecture](../../design/information-architecture.md)                               |
| Plan de course · montre      | Connected watch and « Envoyer »                                                                  | Download of the .fit or enriched GPX                                                                                                                                                          | Direct send waits for the Garmin service (#350, #351)                                                              |
